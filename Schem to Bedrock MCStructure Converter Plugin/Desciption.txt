Schem to Bedrock MCStructure v1.0.0 Plugin

- Converts Java‐format Sponge schematic (.schem) files (v2 & v3) into Bedrock MCStructure (.mcstructure) files.  
- Offers both **bulk‐folder mode** (recursively convert every .schem in a directory) and **file mode** (pick one or more schematics).  
- Prints clean, timestamped logs in the Amulet console per‐file durations, start/end times, and a final success summary.  
- No further updates planned; this tool solves a very specific, niche workflow unless there are bugs.  
- Requirements: Amulet Map Editor with Opened Bedrock World.
